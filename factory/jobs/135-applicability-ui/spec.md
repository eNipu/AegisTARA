## Problem & Goal
Jobs 133 and 134 store conditions and derive exclusions, but nobody can switch a condition, link one, or see why a row or step is excluded. The inspector badge says only "excluded"; owner, via and all-paths reasons are bare words; the canvas, the undo toast, the report and the workbook know nothing of exclusions. A consultant analysing "with and without the Ethernet interface" still cannot do it in the app.

After this job a user can add, rename, switch and remove conditions on a Configuration page (group 1, item definition), link conditions to assets, components, interfaces, threats, trees, goals, claims and steps, and see every exclusion with its reasons: in the inspector banner, in table rows, on canvas nodes (hatched, and as the node's spoken sentence), in the toast after a switch, on the report cover and in a "Configuration and assumptions" report section, and in XLSX columns.

Business value (P7): one switch replaces a copied project per variant, and a reviewer sees what was taken out and why (G50). Each part is what a reviewer or an assessor asks for; the toast tells the editor at once how far one click reached.

## Decisions
- **No model change.** Everything needed (ops, `Applicability`, relation keys) exists. Reason: keeps every storage format untouched and the job a client job.
- **Link columns are hidden by default** on the four tables that exist. Reason: most projects have no condition; a column of empty chips on every table costs width. The chooser still offers them. Components, interfaces, trees and steps have no table yet, so their link UI is the inspector relation row and the picker (job 150).
- **Reasons text is English literals in one function**, like `table.rs` and the toasts in `session.rs` already are. Reason: the table path has no catalog; one function is one place to move to Fluent later. New fixed labels (page title, explainer, cover rows, section) use Fluent in all three locales.
- **Toast reads residual risk through the cache**, as the control-setting toast does; a `ThreatNotApplicable` or `TreeNotApplicable` error is the state `not applicable`. Reason: same numbers as the register.
- **Excluded step count counts leaf nodes only.** Reason: a gate's exclusion follows from its children and would double count.
- **The report section sits after problems, unnumbered like the other late sections.** Reason: numbers 1 to 9 are fixed (a disabled section leaves a gap).
- **Slice order:** a (edits, page, view, columns), b (reasons text, banner, table text, toast), c (canvas), d (report and XLSX). Reason: each needs only the ones before it; c needs b's reasons function.
