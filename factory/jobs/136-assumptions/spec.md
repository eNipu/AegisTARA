## Problem & Goal
Security analysts rely on cybersecurity assumptions to bound the system scope during threat analysis and risk assessment (for example, assuming an OEM backend validates OTA signatures or a bootloader disables debug ports in production), and ISO/SAE 21434 requires tracking which claims depend on each assumption and what attack paths become not applicable while that assumption holds. Without explicit `Assumption` entities integrated into the applicability engine, teams either delete attack trees/steps manually (losing traceability) or maintain assumptions in unstructured notes with no link to claims, risk evaluation, lint warnings, or reports.

Adding `Assumption` (`AS00`, `AS01`, ...) with `holds` toggle, own condition links, excluded attack trees and steps, bidirectional claim links (`CybersecurityClaim::assumptions`), the warning lint `AssumptionWithoutClaim`, an Assumptions page in group 1, inspector `"Excluded while it holds"` editing, and one-click `"Create claim"` draft creation (UI-08) gives analysts a live, undoable, auditable scope switch that updates attack-tree and threat evaluations, surfaces unlinked assumptions, and exports cleanly into Word/XLSX reports and workspace files (P7).

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Model entity & visual ID (`136a`):** `Assumption` (`id: AssumptionId`, visual prefix `VisualPrefix::Assumption` -> `AS00`, `name`, `holds: bool`, `excludes_trees: Vec<TreeIdx>`, `excludes_steps: Vec<StepRef>`, `provenance`), `ElementRef::Assumption(AssumptionId)` descriptions & translations, `EntityKind::Assumption`, and lifecycle ops (`AddAssumption`, `RenameAssumption`, `SetAssumptionHolds`, `RemoveAssumption`) apply, check validation limits (including L15 blank-actor `Provenance` rejection), compute `Op` inverses (`RemoveAssumption` inverse restores name, `holds`, provenance), serialize/deserialize in op-log (`OpWire`/`OpView`), and pass every Element Recipe check.
   Check: `cargo test --locked -p aegis-model --test assumptions` and `cargo test --locked -p aegis-client --test undo_every_op`.
2. **Exclusions, claim links & L30 removal guards (`136b`):**
   - `SetAssumptionExcludes { id, trees, steps, provenance }` replaces `excludes_trees`/`excludes_steps` with sorted, unique validated targets (`Tree` must live; `StepRef { tree, node }` must name a live `Node::Step`), bounded by `trees.len() + steps.len() <= MAX_ASSUMPTION_EXCLUDES` (256), rejecting overflow with `ModelError::TooManyAssumptionExcludes { max: 256 }` and `StoreError::Limit(Limit::ASSUMPTION_EXCLUDES)`, and carrying exact inverse on undo/redo.
   - `LinkClaimAssumption` / `UnlinkClaimAssumption` maintain sorted, unique `CybersecurityClaim::assumptions` (`Vec<AssumptionIdx>`, capped at `MAX_CLAIM_ASSUMPTIONS = 64`).
   - Removal guards enforce L30: `RemoveAssumption` refuses while any claim links it (`AssumptionInUse`), while `excludes` is non-empty (`AssumptionHasExclusions`), while needing conditions (`ElementNeedsConditions`), or while described (`DescriptionNotCleared`); `RemoveCybersecurityClaim` refuses while `assumptions` is non-empty (`CybersecurityClaimHasAssumptions`); `RemoveAttackTree` refuses while any assumption excludes it or any step in it (`AttackTreeExcludedByAssumption`); `RemoveTreeNode` refuses while any assumption excludes `(tree, node)` (`ModelError::NodeIsRated`).
   Check: `cargo test --locked -p aegis-model --test assumptions` and `cargo test --locked -p aegis-client --test undo_every_op`.
3. **Persistence, applicability engine & lint (`136c`):**
   - Snapshots (`assumptions`, `cybersecurity_claims[].assumptions`) and workspace files (`systems/main/assumptions.json` in `WorkspaceFile::LAYOUT`) round-trip losslessly under bounded decoding (`Limit::Assumptions`, `Limit::AssumptionExcludes`, `Limit::ClaimAssumptions`).
   - When `assumption.holds() == true` and no condition linked to `ElementRef::Assumption(id)` is off, every excluded tree gets `Reason::AssumptionHolds(id)` and every excluded step gets `Reason::AssumptionHolds(id)` (propagating through `AND`/`OR`/`SAND` gates to `AllPathsExcluded`, lowering or eliminating attack-tree feasibility and threat risk). Switching `holds` to `false` restores exact pre-toggle feasibility and risk; switching back to `true` excludes them again.
   - When an assumption needs a condition whose `present == false`, `ElementRef::Assumption(id)` is excluded with `Reason::ConditionOff(cid)` and its `holds` switch does NOT exclude its `excludes_trees`/`excludes_steps` while excluded.
   - `Project::applicability()` and `ProjectEvalCache` incremental updates produce identical evaluations in all op application orders (shuffled-order property test).
   Check: `cargo test --locked -p aegis-model --test assumptions`, `cargo test --locked -p aegis-model --test workspace`, and `cargo test --locked -p aegis-model --test ops_properties`.
4. **Lint, client relations, edits, inspector `"Excluded while it holds"` & translations (`136d`):**
   - `LintCode::AssumptionWithoutClaim { assumption }` warns (`LintSeverity::Warning`) when an assumption is not linked by any claim; `lint_claims` does not emit `CybersecurityClaimUnlinked` for a claim that links at least one assumption.
   - `ElementKind::Assumption`, `Selection::Assumption(AssumptionId)`, `TableId::Claims` hidden `"assumptions"` column (`Links(RelationKey::CLAIM_ASSUMPTIONS)`), `RelationKey::{ASSUMPTION_CONDITIONS, ASSUMPTION_EXCLUDES, CLAIM_ASSUMPTIONS}`, and `ELEMENT_CONDITION_KEYS` (8 element kinds) work cleanly through `MultiLinkPicker`, `related`/`reverse_related`, and `InspectorView`.
   - `Edit::{AddAssumption, RenameAssumption, SetAssumptionHolds, RemoveAssumption, SetAssumptionExclude, LinkClaimAssumption}` execute cleanly, and batch removals (`RemoveAssumption`, `RemoveClaim`, `RemoveTreeNode`, `RemoveTree`) plus outline step moves (`tree_diff.rs`) preserve/clear descriptions, conditions, assumption exclusions, and claim links so one undo step restores everything byte-for-byte.
   - Inspector for an assumption shows `"Holds"`, its description, `"Excluded while it holds"` chips + picker (for trees & steps), `"Assumption conditions"`, and reverse `"Claims"` + `"Excluded by"` causes when conditioned off. Tree and step inspectors show reverse `"Excluded by assumptions"`.
   - Trilingual Fluent entries (`locales/{en,ja,pseudo}/main.ftl`, hyphenated IDs) pass all client i18n checks.
   Check: `cargo test --locked -p aegis-model --test assumptions`, `cargo test --locked -p aegis-client --test relations`, `cargo test --locked -p aegis-client --test inspector_view`, `cargo test --locked -p aegis-client --test configuration`, and `cargo test --locked -p aegis-client --test table_conventions`.
5. **Assumptions page, headless `"Create claim"` draft (`UI-08`), exclusion formatting & Slint nav sync (`136e`):**
   - `Page::Assumptions` (`PageLayout::Custom`) sits in `PageGroup::ItemDefinition` (index 4, after `Configuration`), making `Page::ALL.len() == 17`, with `AssumptionsView` (`PageData::assumptions`) and `Page::Assumptions` description/find/nav-count/page_of handling where `AssumptionsView::selected` and `ui.selected_assumption` never diverge.
   - `"Create claim"` (`Command::CreateClaimFromAssumption(id)` or `BeginDraft(DraftKind::Claim)` from `Page::Assumptions` with an assumption selected) opens a claim draft (`ProjectView::draft == Some(DraftKind::Claim)`, `ProjectView::draft_assumption == Some(id)`), creates the claim linked to the assumption in one undo step (`Edit::AddClaimFor`) on `CommitDraft`, and leaves zero ops when cancelled, blank, or given an unknown `AssumptionId`.
   - `exclusion.rs` formats `Reason::AssumptionHolds` in `reasons_text`, includes assumption-excluded elements/steps in `excluded_entries`, and `ConditionSnapshot` captures both `SetConditionPresent` and `SetAssumptionHolds` toasts (emitting 0 excluded steps when conditioned off).
   - `crates/aegis-shell/ui/app.slint` (`global Pages` + `nav-entries`) and `translations/{ja,pseudo}/LC_MESSAGES/aegis-shell.po` stay synced with `Page::ALL`.
   Check: `cargo test --locked -p aegis-client --test assumptions`, `cargo test --locked -p aegis-client --test configuration`, and `cargo test --locked -p aegis-shell --test nav_groups`.
6. **Word and XLSX report integration (`136f`):**
   - Word report (`doc.rs`) adds `"Assumptions"` to the cover table (`report-assumptions` / `report-assumptions-value`), widens `"Configuration and assumptions"` to `has_conditions || has_assumptions` (preserving existing condition-only block indices), appends the `"Assumptions"` table + `Described::Assumptions` descriptions when assumptions exist, and uses trilingual Fluent keys (`en`, `ja`, `pseudo`).
   - Excel report (`xlsx.rs`) uses `has_applicability = has_conditions || has_assumptions` in both `risk_register_sheet` and `report_workbook_xlsx_in`, adds the `"Assumptions"` sheet (`ID`, `Assumption`, `Holds`, `Claims`, `Conditions`, `Excluded while it holds`) when assumptions exist, and escapes formula-injection markers (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab, CR) on assumption fields.
   Check: `scripts/ci.sh` (full workspace), `cargo test --locked -p aegis-client --test assumptions`, and `cargo test --locked -p aegis-client --test report_configuration`.

- Methodology pack customizability for `Assumption` visual prefix or labels (assumption uses the fixed `"AS"` prefix like `"CF"`/`"P"`).
- System scoping (`SystemSet`) on `Assumption` (per-system applicability scoping is future `169-systems-own`; assumptions live in `systems/main/assumptions.json` project-wide like parties/conditions for now).

## Decisions
1. **Use `StepRef { pub tree: TreeId, pub node: NodeId }` instead of `NodeIdx` in `Assumption::excludes_steps`:** `commit_remove_tree_node` calls `AttackTree::remove_leaf` which compacts nodes and shifts subsequent `NodeIdx` values, whereas `(TreeId, NodeId)` stays immutable across sibling edits (matching `review_marks`).
2. **Use `ElementRef::Assumption(AssumptionId)` for assumption descriptions and condition links:** `condition_links` and `descriptions` are already keyed by `ElementRef`, so adding `ElementRef::Assumption` gives localized/translatable descriptions, condition links, report description sections, and removal cleanup without duplicating a separate `description` field on `Assumption` or `AddAssumption`.
3. **Allow `excludes_trees` and `excludes_steps` up to `MAX_ASSUMPTION_EXCLUDES = 256` total (`trees.len() + steps.len() <= 256`) and `MAX_ASSUMPTIONS = 256`, `MAX_CLAIM_ASSUMPTIONS = 64`:** Caps bound decode allocations well below the 64 KiB `OpLog` record cap (~33 KB worst case for `SetAssumptionExcludes`) while fitting realistic automotive TARAs.
4. **Keep `"assumptions"` in `systems/main/assumptions.json` in `WorkspaceFile::LAYOUT`, `assumptions` array inside `CybersecurityClaim` (`shared/claims.json`), and explicit `holds: bool` in `AssumptionView`:** Follows `docs/design/workbench-architecture.md` line 312 for assumptions, mirrors how `CybersecurityClaim::responsible` already serializes inside `ClaimView`, and keeps `holds: true`/`false` explicit in workspace JSON diffs while `AssumptionWire::holds` accepts `#[serde(default)]` (`false`).
5. **Suppress holding assumption exclusions when any condition on `ElementRef::Assumption(id)` is off:** Matches `docs/design/reference-ui-review.md` §3.2 ("an assumption's own conditions switch it off when the condition is off") so inapplicable assumptions do not suppress steps in active configurations.
6. **Warn on `AssumptionWithoutClaim` for every unlinked live assumption; count claim->assumption links in `lint_claims`:** An unlinked assumption lacks claim backing (even if temporarily not holding), and a claim linked only to assumptions (no threats directly) is actively fulfilling an assumption rather than unlinked.
7. **Support `Command::CreateClaimFromAssumption(AssumptionId)` plus `BeginDraft(DraftKind::Claim)` from `Page::Assumptions`:** Gives both headless callers/workbench row actions and keyboard navigation from `Page::Assumptions` a clean draft flow (`ProjectView::draft_assumption`) ending in atomic `Edit::AddClaimFor`, while keeping `AssumptionsView::selected` strictly synced with `ui.selected_assumption`.
8. **Preserve `report_configuration.rs` block indices when zero assumptions exist:** Widen `"Configuration and assumptions"` in `doc.rs` to `has_conditions || has_assumptions`, guard the `"Conditions"` sub-table on `has_conditions`, and append `"Assumptions"` after `"Excluded elements and steps"` only when `has_assumptions` so existing condition-only report assertions remain valid.
9. **Defer `common::record_every_op` entries to `136c` and test ops via `undo_every_op::walk_assumptions` in `136a`/`136b`:** `workspace.rs::round_trip_every_kind` runs snapshot round-trips after every op in `record_every_op`, so keeping `add/remove` out of `record_every_op` until snapshot serialization lands in `136c` keeps every slice green independently.
10. **Split reports (`doc.rs`, `xlsx.rs`, report Fluent keys) into `136f`:** Keeps `136e` and `136f` well below the 600-line slice cap and runs full-workspace CI cleanly on the final slice (`136f`).
