## Facts you need
- The workbook is untrusted input. Nothing here may panic, index out of range, or apply an op that was not validated by the simulation project `sim`.
- A system is an element of kind `aegis_model::ElementKind::System`; `project.elements(ElementKind::System)` yields `(ElementId, &Element)`; `e.name()` is the name. A project holds at most `aegis_model::MAX_SYSTEMS` (64) systems; the model refuses more.
- The model refuses `SetComponentSystem` with `Some(system)` on a component that has a parent (`ModelError::ChildComponentSystem`). The import creates every new component with `parent: None`.
- The workbook this app exports (slice 121b) has a last column "Systems" on sheets that have no component column (risk register, damage scenarios, threats), where the cell may hold several names joined with ", ". Those rows must still import as they do today.
- A row whose ops the simulation refuses goes to `unmapped_rows` with the model's error text; that path exists (lines 1408-1432) and needs no change.
