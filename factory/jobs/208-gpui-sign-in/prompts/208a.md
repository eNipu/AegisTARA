You are implementing one small change in the AegisTARA repository (Rust workspace).

## Goal
The new GPUI app needs the same company, password and development logins the Slint app has, but the credential providers live in the Slint crate. After this slice they live once in `aegis_client::provider`, the Slint app uses them from there, and a new toolkit-free module `aegis_client::signin` turns login options, connection states and login errors into what a sign-in screen shows (stage, login form, translated text). The GPUI start screen (later slices) only renders this.
