## Goal
After this slice `aegis_client::signin::SignIn` does what the Slint launcher does before a project opens, with no toolkit: choose a server, ask it how to log in, log in with a password (optionally kept in the keychain), the company login through the browser, or a development user name, start the `Session`, change server and sign out. A screen calls its methods, calls `pump()` on its own thread, and renders `view()` (a `SignInView` from slice 208a). The GPUI start screen (slice 208e) is built on it.
